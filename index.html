About the ratings

GRADE (high, moderate, low, very low) is the system Cochrane, the WHO and most guidelines use to rate how sure we can be of a finding. When a Cochrane review or guideline has rated the evidence, the card shows their rating and names them. Guideline cards show the guideline's own strength of recommendation.

Not formally graded means no review or guideline has rated it yet, which is common for test cutoffs and change scores. Each study still shows its design and size, so you can judge it yourself.

A reference for clinicians, not medical advice. Confirm numbers against the linked sources before clinical decisions.
